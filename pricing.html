Questions

Practical answers before you choose.

If you are between two plans, pick the one that matches the workflow you need today. You can upgrade as operations grow.

Which plan should a single shop start with?

Starter is free forever for up to 50 inventory items, one owner, and one location. It includes core inventory, stock-in, sales, low-stock visibility, a basic dashboard, and basic sales history. No credit card required.

When should I choose Growth?

Choose Growth when staff accountability, suppliers, stock adjustment approvals, notifications, audit trail, and operational dashboard matter.

Does Starter use up my paid-plan trial?

No. Starter is not a trial. Businesses without previous paid subscriptions or paid-plan trials may qualify for a 14-day trial on their first paid monthly plan. Annual paid plans do not include a trial.

Is Storefront included?

Storefront is Coming Soon in Invenie's App/Plugin Store. Its future pricing and plan availability have not been decided.

Which plan supports branches?

Full Operations is the branch-ready plan. It includes multiple locations, requisitions, issuance, receiving, inter-branch movement, and branch dashboards.

Do annual plans include a discount?

Yes. Growth and Full Operations cost 10% less annually than 12 monthly payments. Starter stays free forever and has no annual billing.
