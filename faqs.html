General

What is Invenie?

Invenie is an inventory management platform for businesses that need clearer stock visibility, product/package pricing, sales workflows, and operational control.

Who is Invenie for?

It is useful for shops, distributors, suppliers, and growing businesses that sell products and need to track stock movement more carefully.

Does Invenie support AI assistance?

Yes. The current plan catalog includes AI assistant access across public plans, helping teams ask operational questions based on their inventory context.

Plans & pricing

Which plans are available?

Invenie currently has Sales Ready Inventory, Operational Inventory, and Full Operations plans, each available monthly or annually.

How much does Invenie cost?

Sales Ready starts at ₦5,000/month, Operational Inventory starts at ₦10,000/month, and Full Operations starts at ₦15,000/month. Annual plans save 10%.

Is there a trial?

Monthly public plans include a 14-day trial. Annual plans are billed once per year.

Which plan supports branches?

Full Operations supports multiple locations, branch management, requisitions, issuance, receiving, transfers, and branch dashboards.

Operations

Can Invenie track stock and sales together?

Yes. Invenie is designed to connect product inventory, package pricing, stock visibility, and sales activity so teams are not working from disconnected records.

Does Invenie support supplier management?

Supplier management is included from Operational Inventory upward, including supplier-linked stock-in workflows.

Can stock adjustments require approval?

Yes. Operational Inventory and Full Operations include stock adjustment workflows, approval settings, audit trail, and notifications.

Can teams have different roles?

User and role management is available from Operational Inventory upward, helping teams separate responsibilities across owners, admins, inventory managers, and sales staff.

Getting started

How do I start?

You can create an account through the Invenie app or schedule a demo if you want help matching your workflow to the right plan.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start with the workflow you need today, then upgrade when your business adds staff accountability, suppliers, approvals, or branches.

Can I ask for help choosing a plan?

Yes. If you are unsure, schedule a demo and share whether your business is single-location, staff-managed, supplier-heavy, or branch-based.
