General

What is Invenie?

Invenie is an inventory management platform for businesses that need clearer stock visibility, product/package pricing, sales workflows, and operational control.

Who is Invenie for?

It is useful for shops, distributors, suppliers, and growing businesses that sell products and need to track stock movement more carefully.

Does Invenie support AI assistance?

Yes. Growth and Full Operations include AI assistant access, helping teams ask operational questions based on their inventory context.

Plans & pricing

Which plans are available?

Invenie offers Starter, Growth, and Full Operations. Starter is free forever for up to 50 inventory items, one owner, and one location. Growth and Full Operations are available monthly or annually.

How much does Invenie cost?

Starter is free forever with no credit card required. Growth costs ₦10,000/month or ₦108,000/year. Full Operations costs ₦15,000/month or ₦162,000/year. Annual paid plans save 10%.

Is there a trial?

Starter is free forever, not a trial, and does not consume paid-plan trial eligibility. Businesses without previous paid subscriptions or paid-plan trials may qualify for a 14-day trial on their first Growth or Full Operations monthly plan. Annual paid plans are billed once per year without a trial.

Which plan supports branches?

Full Operations supports multiple locations, branch management, requisitions, issuance, receiving, transfers, and branch dashboards.

Operations

Can Invenie track stock and sales together?

Yes. Invenie is designed to connect product inventory, package pricing, stock visibility, and sales activity so teams are not working from disconnected records.

Does Invenie support supplier management?

Supplier management is included from Growth upward, including supplier-linked stock-in workflows.

Can stock adjustments require approval?

Yes. Growth and Full Operations include stock adjustment workflows, approval settings, audit trail, and notifications.

Can teams have different roles?

User and role management is available from Growth upward, helping teams separate responsibilities across owners, admins, inventory managers, and sales staff.

Getting started

How do I start?

You can Start Free with Starter through the Invenie app or schedule a demo if you want help matching your workflow to the right plan.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start with the workflow you need today, then upgrade when your business adds staff accountability, suppliers, approvals, or branches.

Can I ask for help choosing a plan?

Yes. If you are unsure, schedule a demo and share whether your business is single-location, staff-managed, supplier-heavy, or branch-based.
